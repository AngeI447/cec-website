01 — Power Transmission & Distribution

Carrying power across Rwanda and beyond

Design, build, and commission the infrastructure that carries power across Rwanda and beyond — from the country's first 220kV line to last-mile distribution networks.

  • ▪ High-voltage transmission lines up to 220kV
  • ▪ Medium and low-voltage distribution networks
  • ▪ Substations, feeders and transformer upgrades
  • ▪ Street lighting and productive-user connections

Selected projects: Shango–Birembo 220kV line (Rwanda–DRC), Mukungwa–Nyabihu 110kV, Kigali Ring 110kV, Nyabarongo 110/30kV substation, Huye–Gisagara street lights.

02 — Power Generation Infrastructure

Building and renewing generation capacity

Design, construction, rehabilitation, and upgrade of hydropower plants and related generation facilities.

  • ▪ Hydropower plant civil works and structures
  • ▪ Rehabilitation and capacity upgrades
  • ▪ Electromechanical installation and commissioning
  • ▪ Access roads and balance-of-plant infrastructure

Selected projects: Keya HPP (2.2 MW), Nkora HPP (0.68 MW) and Cyimbili HPP (0.3 MW) — 3.18 MW combined rehabilitation and upgrade.

03 — Marine & Civil Construction

Opening new trade routes on Lake Kivu

Ports, jetties, quays, and offshore structures. Landmark work includes Rwanda's first commercial port on Lake Kivu.

  • ▪ Quay and jetty construction, including offshore works
  • ▪ Passenger terminals and cargo storage
  • ▪ Port utilities, power substations and road networks
  • ▪ Marine and heavy civil structures

Selected projects: Port of Rubavu, Port of Rusizi, Karongi Auxiliary Quay.

04 — Real Estate & Housing

Shaping the cities of tomorrow

Residential, commercial, and mixed-use development — apartment complexes, hotels, private residences, and community projects.

  • ▪ Apartment complexes and residential housing
  • ▪ Hotels and hospitality developments
  • ▪ Private residences and mixed-use buildings
  • ▪ Full architectural and structural delivery

Selected projects: Kisima Luxury Apartments (64 units, 8 blocks), Trinity International Academy, Lebanon Hotel Remera, EPC Africa Group HQ, Akagera Game Lodge, Residential House Kimihurura.

Infrastructure that anchors communities

Schools, public facilities, and civic infrastructure that anchor communities.

The systems that make buildings and plants work
