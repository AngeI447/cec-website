About CEC

Building the Infrastructure That Carries Rwanda Forward

Century Engineering Contractors (CEC Ltd) is a Rwandan civil and electromechanical contractor and a member of EPC Africa Group. Since 2014 we have delivered power, ports, hydropower, water and buildings across the country.

Our Story

A decade of engineering delivery

Since 2014, Century Engineering Contractors has delivered the projects that carry Rwanda forward — power transmission lines that connect the country to its neighbours, ports that open new trade routes on Lake Kivu, hydropower plants that light homes and industries, and housing and public buildings that shape the cities of tomorrow.

We work at the intersection of civil engineering and electromechanical execution, delivering value for money, quality, and safety on every site. As a member of EPC Africa Group, CEC combines local delivery with the strength of a wider engineering group.
