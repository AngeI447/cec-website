About CEC

Civil and electromechanical contractors, and a member of EPC Africa Group.

Civil and Electromechanical Contractors Building for Africa

Since 2014, Century Engineering Contractors has delivered the projects that carry Rwanda forward. Power transmission lines that connect the country to its neighbours. Ports that open new trade routes on Lake Kivu. Hydropower plants that light homes and industries. Housing and public buildings that shape the cities of tomorrow. We work at the intersection of civil engineering and electromechanical execution, delivering value for money, quality, and safety on every site.

2014 Year Founded A decade of engineering delivery in Rwanda.
20+ Projects Delivered Across energy, marine, buildings and utilities.
140 Employees Engineers, technicians and skilled site teams.
